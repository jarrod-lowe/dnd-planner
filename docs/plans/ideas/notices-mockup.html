Notices: which layout?

Notices are short passive reminders derived from character facts — things a player must remember that are not consequences of a choice made this turn. The section sits between Active States and This Turn I Want To…, so it is squeezed: every row it takes is a row the plan stack loses.

The same five real notices are rendered four ways below, each in place in the panel. You are choosing how much of a notice is visible at rest: only its label (A, C), label and full body (B), or label plus a clipped body (D) — and whether source is a prefix, a column, or a grouping. Watch items 4 and 5: their payload (a DC, a recurring roll) lives in the body, so any variant that hides bodies hides the thing the player actually needs. Flip the theme with the button top-right, and try the page at tablet width and at 400px.

Variant A — Wrapping chips, tap to expand

Cheapest at rest (two rows for five notices) but every body costs a tap; the expanded chip claims a whole row, so the section height jumps as the player reads.
